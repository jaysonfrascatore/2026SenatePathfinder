Forecast Methodology

Raw Paths counts all 256 equal-weight scenarios — each contested seat is a pure 50/50 coin flip with no polling information applied.

Poll Forecast converts each state's poll margin to a win probability via a logistic curve (σ=8), then multiplies across all 8 states to get each scenario's true probability share. Scenarios are sorted by likelihood; those below 0.5% share are forecast-eliminated and shown faded with ✕.

Env Shift applies a uniform swing to every state's margin when a bellwether result is entered. Competitive states absorb the full swing; safe states absorb ~60%.
